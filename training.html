Easy Runs

Easy runs build endurance and help your body recover between harder workouts.

Interval Training

Intervals combine fast running with recovery periods to improve speed and fitness.

Long Runs

Long runs develop endurance and prepare runners for longer distances.

Training and Preparation

Training is one of the most important parts of becoming a better runner. It takes consistency, patience, and hard work to improve and reach new goals.

Running workouts can include easy runs, intervals, long runs, and strength training. Rest and recovery are also important because they help the body adapt and become stronger.
